- [ ] Research PDF generated, rendered, visually inspected
- [ ] Devpost fields, team mapping, code links complete
- [ ] Resolve video requirement conservatively
- [ ] Final Kaggle selections verified (maximum two)
- [ ] Devpost actually submitted and reopened for verification
